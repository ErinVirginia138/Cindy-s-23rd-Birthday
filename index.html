A Letter For Cindy

To The Friend Who Grew Up With Me

Dear Cindy,

Happy 23rd Birthday. 🤍

It honestly feels a little strange realizing how long we've known each other.

We met when we were still in high school. Back then, our biggest worries were homework, exams, and our academy results.

We never imagined that one day we'd be talking about work, responsibilities, and trying to figure out adulthood.

Somewhere along the way, life became busier.

We don't talk every day. Sometimes days turn into weeks. Sometimes weeks become months.

But somehow...

Every time we meet again, it feels like we simply continue a conversation that was only paused.

There's never any awkward silence.

We still laugh at the smallest things. We still end up talking for hours. We still somehow have endless stories to tell.

I think that's the beautiful thing about a friendship like ours.

Some friendships need constant conversations.

Ours never did.

Because no matter how much time passes, seeing you always feels familiar.

Thank you...

For every memory.

For every laugh.

For every random conversation.

For every time we met after months as if nothing had changed.

Life has changed so much.

We've both grown up.

But I'm really grateful that one thing never changed—

having you as one of the people I get to grow up with.

I don't know where life will take us five or ten years from now.

Maybe we'll become busier. Maybe we'll meet less often.

But I hope every time we finally meet, we'll still laugh just as much as we always do.

Thank you...

for growing up with me 🤍

With love,

Erika 🤍
